# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-06

First release.

- A clock app for the BUSY Bar, inspired by a famous game.
- Seconds on or off, with four animation styles.
- Five colours, 12h or 24h, five speeds, colon blinking, steady or off.
- Tap the wheel to switch the animation on or off, turn it to change the colour.
